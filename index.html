About Me

I am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Computer Science at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), where my research at the Vision and Robotics Laboratory (VeRLab) focuses on collaborative perimeter defense strategies under the supervision of Professor Douglas Guimarães Macharet. I earned a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from the Pontifícia Universidade Católica de Minas Gerais (PUC Minas), with a thesis titled "Prioritizing Individuals for COVID-19 Testing Under Resource Scarcity," advised by Professor Luiz Alberto Ferreira Gomes. During my undergraduate studies in Computational Mathematics at UFMG, I developed a solid foundation in analytical and computational problem-solving skills. My research interests span autonomous agents, multi-robot systems, dynamic vehicle routing, and combinatorial optimization.

Adaptive Team Formation for Perimeter Defense in Multi-Robot Systems (2023-today)
The Perimeter Defense problem is a particular case in the application of multi-robot systems, since we have the presence of adversarial agents, that is, agents that act against the success of the entire mission.

Computer Vision

Computer Vision solution to construction site (2023-today)

The Multiview System enables the monitoring of construction and manufacturing processes on industrial sites using computer vision. This project focuses on investigating techniques, models, and algorithms to develop a system for the automatic semantic labeling of elements identified in point clouds. It aims to create innovative solutions for point cloud analysis.

Funding: Petrobras (Brazil).
Local: DCC - UFMG (Brazil).
