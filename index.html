About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate student at UFMG researching multi-robot perimeter defense at VeRLab DCC-UFMG. Skilled in full-stack development, robotics, and data science. Passionate about digital art, chess, and team sports like futsal and volleyball.

Current Research

Artificial Intelligence

Adaptive Team Formation for Perimeter Defense in Multi-Robot Systems (2023-today)
The Perimeter Defense problem is a particular case in the application of multi-robot systems, since we have the presence of adversarial agents, that is, agents that act against the success of the entire mission.


Funding: CNPq (Brazil).
Local: DCC - UFMG (Brazil).

Computer Vision

Computer Vision solution to construction site (2023-today)

The Multiview System enables the monitoring of construction and manufacturing processes on industrial sites using computer vision. This project focuses on investigating techniques, models, and algorithms to develop a system for the automatic semantic labeling of elements identified in point clouds. It aims to create innovative solutions for point cloud analysis.

Funding: Petrobras (Brazil).
Local: DCC - UFMG (Brazil).
